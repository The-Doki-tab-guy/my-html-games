System Override Codes

Enter the following commands into the in-game developer console (Press ` to open) and hit Enter.

Command Effect
godmode or iddqd Toggles invincibility. You will take 0 damage from entity collisions.
ammo or idkfa Toggles infinite ammunition. Ammo counter will display as ∞.
heal Instantly restores health to maximum capacity (100%).
sonic Toggles extreme movement speed (2.5x standard velocity).
spawn Forces a new enemy entity to spawn randomly on the map.
help Opens this database document.
