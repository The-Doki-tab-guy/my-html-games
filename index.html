Basic Combat

WASD to Move (Infantry).
SPACE to Jump.
SHIFT to Dash (directional).
LEFT CLICK to Shoot.
1 - 2 - 3 - 4 to select weapons (V-Blaster, Heavy Pulse, Railgun, Plasma Shotgun).
` (Tilde) to open Cheat Console.
ESC to pause the run.
E to interact with Shop Terminals, Cars, or Planes.

Weapons

V-BLASTER: Rapid fire automatic laser rifle. Hold left click.
HEAVY PULSE: Slower, hard-hitting energy projectile.
RAILGUN: Extreme damage precision beam with long recharge.
PLASMA SHOTGUN: Close-range spread blast, devastating against swarms.

Difficulty

EASY: Fewer, weaker enemies. More starting credits.
NORMAL: Standard Overdrive balance.
HARD: Tougher, faster enemies and bigger waves for bigger credit payouts.

Vehicles (Cars)

Look at a vehicle and press E to enter.
W / S to Accelerate / Brake (Reverses Signs caught by pilot).
A / D to Steer.
SPACE for Hydraulic Jump.
Press E again to exit.

Aircraft (Planes)

Look at an aircraft and press E to enter.
SPACE to Increase Thrust.
SHIFT to Decrease Thrust.
S = Pitch UP (Climb).
W = Pitch DOWN (Dive).
A / D = Roll Left / Right (Yaw turns automatically while banking).
LEFT CLICK fires primary aircraft weapons.
Press E again to exit.
