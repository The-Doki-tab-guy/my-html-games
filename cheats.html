System Override Codes

Enter the following commands into the in-game developer console (Press ` to open) and hit Enter.

Command Effect
godmode or iddqd Toggles invincibility. You will take 0 damage from entity collisions.
ammo or idkfa Toggles infinite ammunition. Ammo counter will display as INF.
noclip Allows the player to fly freely and pass through collisions (Use Space/Shift).
money or rich Instantly deposits 10,000 CR into your bank account.
car Spawns a drivable Synth-Vehicle directly in front of your current position.
help Opens this database document.
